Hover-to-play: three ways

Same film, same chapters, no play button in any of them. Hover to preview, move away to pause on the exact frame, hover back to resume. One click commits to watching with sound.

Why hover-play is muted: browsers block audible playback that a click didn't start — hovering doesn't count as a gesture. So hover gives a silent preview, and the single click that unmutes is also the click that hands you real controls. That is what removes the old play-then-pause double click.

A

Preview & Commit

The literal version of what you asked for. Hover plays, leaving pauses and holds the frame (no poster flash), returning resumes. Click anywhere unmutes and switches to native controls — after that, moving the mouse away no longer pauses it.

B

Scrub Rail

A, plus the "play around in it" part. A rail appears along the bottom while previewing: sweep across it and you scrub the whole 2:28 by cursor position, with the chapter under your cursor named as you go. Ticks mark chapter boundaries. Leave the rail and the preview resumes from wherever you landed.

C

Chapter Deck

The frame splits into the six chapters the film already has. Hovering a band jumps to that chapter and previews it; the band you're over is named. Click enters that chapter with sound. This turns the whole poster into a table of contents rather than a single play target.
