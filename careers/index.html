How I work

Real experience only
Every line on your resume and every interview answer is built from what you've actually done, studied or built. I won't add employers, titles, dates or numbers you can't back up. They fall apart in the second follow-up question, and employers check.
Your details stay private
Your resume and personal details are never published. The samples on this site are fictional, or used with all personal details removed.
I coach; you perform
I don't sit interviews, take assessments or do your job for you, and I don't feed answers during a live interview.
You keep everything
Study pages, workbooks, question banks and recordings are yours.

Frequently asked questions

Can you guarantee me a job?

No. Nobody honestly can. What I can do is make sure your resume is credible, you understand the job you're interviewing for, and you can explain your own experience clearly.

I have no supply chain experience. Can you still help?

Yes. Many people moving in already have related experience, such as data work, reporting, operations or purchasing. We present that honestly and add a real portfolio project to show the planning skills.

Which roles do you prepare people for?

Demand planner, supply planner, inventory planner or analyst, S&OP analyst, supply chain analyst and procurement analyst.

Which countries?

Sessions are online. My materials are built for US and Gulf job markets; if you're targeting another market, tell me on the call.

How are sessions run?

Live and online; we confirm the platform on the call. Prep materials are shared as web pages, Excel files and notes you keep.

Will you write my resume with more experience than I have?

No. See "How I work". It's also the fastest way to fail an interview.

What does it cost?

It depends on what you need and your timeline. We'll go through options on the free call.
