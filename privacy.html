Privacy

Privacy notice

This portfolio uses limited analytics and two voluntary forms. It does not sell personal information or use it for automated hiring decisions.

Information this site processes

  • Site usage: Google Analytics 4 may process device, browser, approximate location, referral and interaction information.
  • Contact messages: when you submit the contact form, Formspree processes the name, email, purpose and message you choose to provide.
  • Newsletter requests: when you request The Corridor, MailerLite processes the email address you provide and manages delivery or subscription preferences.

Why it is used

Usage information helps identify slow or confusing pages and measure whether portfolio tools are useful. Contact and newsletter details are used only to deliver the requested response or resource and related communications you have chosen.

Analytics and cookies

Google Analytics may store or read identifiers in your browser, subject to your browser settings and Google’s controls. Portfolio measurement events are designed not to send form text, names, email addresses or phone numbers. You can block analytics with browser privacy controls or an analytics-blocking extension.

Service providers and retention

Google Analytics, Formspree and MailerLite act as service providers for their respective functions and apply their own security and retention practices. Information is kept only as long as reasonably needed to review site performance, reply to an enquiry or provide a requested newsletter, subject to provider settings and legal obligations.

Your choices

You may ask what information you voluntarily submitted, request correction or deletion, or withdraw from newsletter communications using the unsubscribe option in an email. To make a request, email vinayakbhadani@zohomail.in.

Updates

This notice may change when the portfolio’s tools or providers change. The current version was last updated on 31 August 2026.
